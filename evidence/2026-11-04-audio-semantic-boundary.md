2026-11-04 UTC. Astra integration at a0a851d803227accfb7f5c9483379e58e4fe3040; clean branch matched fetched origin before work. [Ledger](../milestones/README.md) owns status. Source/design finding only: resolved=false, authoritative=0; M1.13, formal M4.1 and integrated controls remain open.

## Decision and exact blocker

No runtime correction is selected. The exact prerequisite is a joint checkpoint/reconstruction contract for logical AudioOut occurrences, event/wait outcomes and guest worker progress, with causal identity for irreversible presentation. The present source supplies neither this joint cut nor a corrected audio commit boundary. A FIFO copy, device reset, event reset, replay mute or page exclusion cannot discharge it. This is a bounded negative source result, not a request to repeat the accepted thirteen-getter observation.

A playing occurrence is one accepted append in a stream lifetime; a released occurrence is its published, not yet returned logical reuse credit. Pointer reuse is not occurrence identity. Each logical occurrence must occupy exactly one state: pending, released, returned, or explicitly retired by a specified lifecycle rule. Restore must reinstate checkpoint logical ownership, not spend future original-branch credits. Physical device storage and already emitted output remain externally owned and cannot rewind.

The conditional implementation boundary is logical AudioOut service versus physical presenter; no algorithm or backend replacement is adopted here. Before implementation, specify completion eligibility and guest work ordering for both unchanged replay and changed-input correction. An old getter-result tape indexed by poll ordinal can diagnose unchanged replay but cannot define corrected execution. If the existing native wait/worker boundary cannot meet the invariant, apply ADR-0007 reconsideration with a separate architecture decision; do not weaken coverage.

## Invariant and required boundary

For a valid checkpoint and identical causal inputs under a specified deterministic service/work order, restore and replay must reproduce required guest state, logical release/null and API/wait outcomes, producer decisions and consumer progress at declared boundaries, independently of later physical device progress. A weaker equivalence requires a noninterference proof that is currently absent. Checkpoint storage must remain immutable; native headers/PCM stay valid until actual device ownership ends, independent of guest descriptor reuse.

Presentation requires stable causal effect identities across replay, stream and match lifetimes. An unchanged effect may be emitted at most once; distinct legitimate equal-PCM or same-pointer effects must not be collapsed. Wrong predictions invalidate uncommitted effects. Already heard speculative sound cannot be undone and needs an explicit policy. WAV/hash writes and lifecycle teardown are effects too. Delaying presentation must not delay logical release/work until device playback and thereby change guest progress.

Confirmation is an alternative for external output only after causal tagging exists. rbn_confirmed is an input receipt horizon, not a generic corrected audio commit API. Commit requires actual inputs, applied corrections and completed required work through every effect's latest dependency. An asynchronous 512-sample chunk cannot simply inherit the current battle frame. Deferring the entire guest audio worker instead requires a stronger noninterference proof and architectural review.

1. Preserve the three-released/two-pending checkpoint shape. Require coherent capture/restore or refusal before guest/history mutation; unexpected post-mutation failure must fail-stop. Exercise reset/unprepare/write failures under an explicit lifetime policy.
3. Assert guest queue P/C, payloads, accept/full decisions and consumer completions separately. Matching getters with persistent six-versus-four progress falsifies a release-only complete correction. Equal occupancy, submissions or scratch triples cannot waive this test.
4. Compare unchanged and changed-input executions to uninterrupted oracles. Test duplicate suppression plus two legitimate identical payloads, removed predicted effects, newly corrected effects and stream reopen. An ordinal tape failing changed-input correspondence remains diagnostic only.

First failure selects the next seam: ownership/cut, wait/service progression, independent guest-work ordering, or effect identity. No broad new observer is required to classify the retained run. The missing logical-work schedule and causal correspondence are explicit inputs to this test, not assumptions that the current receipts already prove.
