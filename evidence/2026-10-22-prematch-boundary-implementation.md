## Policy and record semantics

Start preserves LastError including around getenv; enabled boundaries preserve it around all validation, checked reads, printf and fflush. The only guest read is a 16-byte read of four uint32 words at actual address `71052391c0`, through the unchanged, nonpublishing-on-failure `fh_reset_read`. Counter checks also follow the checked read to detect an observed concurrent selector entry. Supported shape is exactly step 15, Z, hold 8, wait 90 or 150, nonnegative tick, same thread/wait and exact interval.

Success emits exactly two ordered `[rng-menu] sample version=1` records with phase, tick, wait, thread, validated zero counters/fighters and four RNG words. No startup success record is needed. Bad prerequisites, phase/order, restart, shape, interval/thread, state or checked read emit one `[rng-menu] unusable version=1 reason=...` record and latch terminal suppression. This deliberately makes the observation unusable without aborting or altering gameplay. Repeated calls after successful completion emit one terminal order diagnostic, so a previously complete pair cannot conceal misuse. There are at most two samples and one unusable diagnostic per process. Failure after begin leaves an incomplete rejected pair; read failure publishes no partial/zero RNG sample. A process that never reaches the boundary has no complete pair and is rejected by analysis.

The sequence caller must be serialized, as in the agreed integration. Same-thread checks detect a changed caller but do not provide synchronization for simultaneous calls. Counter checks and the four-word read are non-atomic with guest activity; no absence of concurrent RNG writers, executed-store attribution or precise call count follows. Logging can perturb scheduling; a matching pair cannot establish all state equality. No wall-clock duration is claimed.
