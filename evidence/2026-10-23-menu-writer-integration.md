## Decision

M3.5's early forward divergence is attributed to a fixture cause (tape-dependent legitimate consumption), not to a runtime writer, seed or reset defect. Stop this experiment. Next: specify the justified fixture treatment (a reference is comparable only under an identical pre-fight tick history; record the pre-fight consumption count as part of the fixture identity) and only then return to the restoration gates. Writer instrumentation remains a forward-only diagnostic; it does not qualify rollback, restoration or complete-state equality.
