## Implementation and ownership

Only `runtime/rb_learned.c` and new protection_observation tests are changed. The observer is opt-in through exact environment value `SSBU_LS_PROTECTION_OBSERVE=1`, read by an explicit initialization interface. It records only the first `ls_protect_all` after initialization, not later bases, decay, faults, commits, teardown or other VirtualProtect sites.

Fixed process-lifetime host storage holds at most 4,096 range rows and the first bulk and first fallback-page failure. No new allocation, lock, environment read or logging occurs in the held recording path. There are at most two additional VirtualQuery calls. Setup runs under the existing closed learned callback gate; this gate is not proof that all guest/native/host writers are stopped. Failure query state is checked after the failed call, and may differ from state at failure. The query validates a complete first page within the returned region; a bulk record does not claim its entire attempted span is one region. No guest page is dereferenced for this diagnostic.

Each range records raw start/length, byte intersection with both windows, the unchanged production acceptance result, and invalid geometry. The end row records completeness/truncation and excluded, attempted, successful and failed page-attempt totals plus bulk failures. Counters are setup attempts, not independently deduplicated page coverage. Exclusions count only accepted ranges visited by the production walk; skipped intersections are not classified as authoritative or eligible pages. The two failure rows are mandatory even when no failure occurred.

The producer seals immutable records with InterlockedExchange before publishing its existing gate. The drain claims a sealed record once with InterlockedCompareExchange; later setups do not overwrite it. No buffer reset or rewind ownership is introduced. Initialization and drain preserve entry GetLastError; the protection wrapper preserves the underlying call's post-call GetLastError across the query. Existing logging and other pre-existing calls may independently alter last error, so there is no whole-setup last-error promise. Disabled recording makes no query and adds no protection calls.

## Root integration contract

Root owns `runtime/r1_state.c`, `runtime/r1_ring.c` and `runtime/hostrt.h`; none was edited here. Required interfaces are:

- `void r1_protection_observation_init(void);` Call once from serialized runtime startup before any learned protection setup or native/worker holds. This is deliberately not wired into `ls_init`, preserving existing startup/hook extraction fixtures. Without this call the observer remains disabled, even if the environment variable is set.
- `void r1_protection_observation_flush(void);` Call on the serialized control thread after **all** native and worker holds have ended, on success and refusal paths. A premature call before records are sealed does nothing and can be retried. A call after sealing logs at most 4,100 rows once. Root must establish the release boundary from caller source, not infer it from learned-gate publication. Do not add logging under holds to get output before base publication: data are sampled during setup; output is deferred. A normal shutdown fallback may be useful but cannot replace refusal-path draining.
