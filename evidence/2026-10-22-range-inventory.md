## Changes and semantic scope

`add_range` now rejects noncontiguous capacity exhaustion and address overflow, while allowing extension of the last contiguous range at capacity. Large-heap discovery records sticky incompleteness beyond eight entries. Stack-exclusion capture rejects its 512-entry limit and arithmetic overflow. `collect` reports failure when exclusions need an unavailable split slot. It releases and clears old range ownership before rebuilding, preventing freed `dirty`/`async` pointers or borrowed learned pointers from following reused/sorted slots. Sorted overlapping ranges now retain their complete union rather than silently dropping an extending tail.

Initial save, diagnostic mark save and rebase refuse incomplete collection. The ring and run-ahead callers recognize the explicit inventory failure, and diagnostic mark-save allocations are checked before holds or copy. Rebase retains its prior contract: old-match history has already been retired, so failure disables the unusable ring without publishing a new base and releases its hold. It does not promise old-history preservation.

Compared with pinned upstream `c7c874977ea7510c291ec8c278a58883a2f4e368`, these are deliberate complete-or-refuse failure-policy changes under ADR-0007. Successful range union preserves all requested bytes, including overlapping tails previously omitted. No claim of complete process memory or native quiescence follows from these bounds checks. OS enumeration failure handling, cached heap discovery, extras and native inventories remain separately open.
