## Lifecycle and mode boundaries

Every first-player record hook invokes hs_apply again, so a successful rematch negotiates/publishes a fresh seed and applies fresh setup inputs. `rbn_match_reset` (`rb_net.c:795..809`) resets session/history bookkeeping but does not clear g_hs_seed; rbn_match_seed can therefore retain the prior agreement between matches. Do not authorize a new seed-input write merely because rbn_match_seed is nonzero. Bind it to successful current setup, and exclude failed/fallback and ordinary local/Local Wireless cases. This is a concrete stale-seed hazard for a proposed patch, not evidence that the current owner copy is stale.

Network mode distinctions matter: normal host/join handshake, loop mode (no handshake; optional loop seed only), handshake-disabled mode, failed handshake fallback, local-with-no-room, and Local Wireless bypass are distinct paths (`rb_net.c:152..164`, 269..310). Local Wireless and local bypass intentionally leave original setup intact. An offline flag change cannot qualify these modes.

Original owner seed and generator vectors reside in guest state; restoration must restore their checkpoint values, not rerun initialization or reseed from the current host handshake. `r1_ring.c` describes image/module pages plus extras, but coverage/quiescence remain M7 obligations. No explicit rematch clearing of g_fh_n was found in fighter_host or runtime sources: first-fighter reseeding is keyed to first registration, not an independently validated per-match initialization lifecycle. Snapshot treatment of host bookkeeping/random-byte stream is not established by this report. These are validation constraints; do not expand this finite seed audit into a replacement snapshot subsystem.
