Direct observation in enabled A: the first-frame fixed words survive both fighters' original callbacks. Between **after slot 1/frame 1** and **before slot 0/frame 2**, all nine generators change to identical four-word states `812cb3b5,00d0d634,e0725686,370c4f7c`, with all counters zero. No further change occurs through the sampled third callbacks. Those 180 bytes reproduce reference hash `9d723f07`. The four words satisfy `next = 1812433253 * (word XOR (word >> 30)) + i`, modulo 2^32 for i=1..3. This is evidence of state replacement in that interval, consistent with reseeding after diagnostic fixed-state initialization; it does not identify the executing writer.

The older failing SDK runs log fourth word `b2108a6e` for unused generators before fixed initialization. Inverting that recurrence yields `8f1a16d7,e755ff0a,0fa86f8f,b2108a6e`; nine copies with zero counters reproduce failing hash `da6e5394`. `seed-reconstruction.json` records the calculation. This is an algebraic reconstruction, not a full raw-state observation of those failures, and matching a folded hash alone is not proof of unique state or causality.
