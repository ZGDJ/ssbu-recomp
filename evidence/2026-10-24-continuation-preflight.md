## Invariant and caller trace

Before ring history/page mutation, each required saved continuation must be present under the expected identity and at its saved wait. After mutation, unexpected incompleteness must terminate before unpark or success. The inherited `r1_ring_load` checked running/busy counters, but `ra_hold_restore` accepts wrong blocked waits without running, and its audio fast path can return same=0 without incrementing mismatch. Repeating the hold can set same=1 on an already held wrong-wait thread. Therefore aggregate flags alone cannot establish a matching continuation.

`guest_thread_info` returns the current Obj handle/context, or a null handle for finished threads. Inventory count is monotonic but does not detect Obj completion/handle replacement. `ra_restore_threads_valid` checks saved handle/context pairs against every current active thread, live handle status, complete membership and duplicate/missing entries. Snapshot metadata may not pretend to own live holds. New registration since the snapshot is conservatively unsupported and refused; the old strategy of permanently suspending born threads is not retirement proof. Existing born-hold cleanup still handles growth during the preparation race.

The ring calls this check before copying saved thread metadata, after holds, just before destructive mutation and before successful resume. Held checks require same/held/cok, valid host context observations, writable/readable guest context, matching import/return address and saved guest SP. Already-held transfer additionally requires matching context identity. The explicit existing nopus decoder exclusion remains identified and is not promoted into a quiescence claim. Holding at a wrong wait is never relabeled as correct to pass validation.

Fiber preparation continues to use production `fibers_prepare` and `fiber_restore_action` for missing/busy/live/unsupported continuation refusal. `fibers_prepared_valid` rechecks required current identities against the inventory used by preparation immediately before destructive mutation. Prepared commit now reads back fiber object identity/fields and FLS ownership instead of counting a silent missing-object put as success. A final ring completion guard rejects thread mismatch, incomplete fiber counts or changed held continuations through the fatal path before unpark; the old success-path mismatch log is removed.

`restore`, `fast_restore`, `mark_restore` and `ra_restore` still have separate legacy continuation paths; do not attribute the ring guard to them. Their disposition is required before whole-M7.1 acceptance or default legacy replay qualification. M7.3 remains responsible for what the correction coordinator does with refusal, and M7.2 for terminal lifetime. The supported-path restrictions must be explicit before those gates close.
