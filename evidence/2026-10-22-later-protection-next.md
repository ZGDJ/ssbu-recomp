## Exact uncertainty and smallest useful observation

A failed bulk call that fully recovers through fallback does not increment this counter. Fatal fault/host-write/committed-page transitions do not explain a normal nonfatal base refusal under the inspected code. `ls_decommit_learn` can continue after `ls_open` failure only if its checked subsequent page query establishes reserve/free. Its failed opening already increments the sticky counter. Hence the surviving candidates are setup fallback failures, opening failures followed by verified absence on the decommit path, or both. The old log cannot assign the 177 increments to those sites or recover their addresses/OS errors.
