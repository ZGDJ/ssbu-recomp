## Invariant and decision

Missing/zero/refused restore must never fall through to another capture, forward pass or finalization. Transient negative refusal is no longer retried: the first refusal ends the session, so repeated refusal cannot silently continue or loop forever. This trades availability for explicit correctness until recovery is proven. ADR-0007/FSD-005/FSD-008 permit this fail-stop disposition. No accepted gameplay trajectory is changed by a successful correction.
